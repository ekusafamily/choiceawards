DeKUTSO

The Director of Students Welfare facilitates the election of students leaders in conjunction with the Schools Committee.The duly elected officials are taken through leadership training after which they are expected to prepare their annual budget and submit it to the management for approval. DeKUTSO constitution was promulgated on 31st August, 2015.

The current office holders of DeKUTSO are:

Moses Musyoka Mutuku - Chairperson

Moses Musyoka Mutuku is a dynamic and visionary student leader currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Telecommunication and Information Engineering. Renowned for his exceptional leadership, Acumen, and Acuity, he exceptionally serves as the Chairperson of the Dedan Kimathi University Students’ Organisation (DeKUTSO). Previously, He has held different leadership positions such as Delegate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering College, and Association Leadership.

A distinguished scholar and tech Guru, Moses blends academic excellence with hands-on experience in IoT, cloud computing, digital innovation, and modern engineering systems. His professional journey includes impactful roles at Konza Technopolis and SCINOV Africa.

As Chairperson, Moses champions a transformative agenda centred on student empowerment, institutional collaboration, and sustainable development. His leadership style is defined by accountability, inclusivity, and an unwavering dedication to advancing the welfare, voice, and opportunities of all students. He believes a university thrives when every Voice is heard, every Talent is nurtured and every Dream finds room to grow.

Driven, articulate, and purpose-led, Moses stands as a model of excellence — inspiring a culture of innovation, resilience, and progress within the DeKUT community and beyond.

Pascoline Muthoni Wereri - Vice Chairperson

Pascoline Muthoni Wereri is a distinguished second-year Nursing student at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Nursing. Driven by a deep passion for healthcare, service, and community advancement, Pascoline has established herself as a dedicated scholar and an emerging leader whose actions reflect integrity, empathy, and excellence.

Since joining the university, Pascoline has consistently demonstrated a strong commitment to academic achievement, student welfare, and collaborative problem-solving. Her involvement in student initiatives and her ability to work effectively with diverse groups of people have shaped her into a grounded and visionary leader with a clear sense of purpose.

In 2025, Pascoline was elected as the Vice President of the Dedan Kimathi University of Technology Student Council (2025/2026) a role that underscores her credibility, competence, and unwavering resolve to champion the needs of her fellow students. In this capacity, she advocates for inclusive governance, enhanced academic support structures, improved welfare systems, and an enriched campus experience for every student.

Guided by her background in Nursing, Pascoline brings a unique blend of compassion and strategic leadership to university governance. She is committed to creating a student-centered environment where each individual feels supported, heard, and empowered to reach their full potential. Her priorities include strengthening communication between students and administration, promoting mental and physical wellness, and supporting initiatives that enhance academic and professional development.

Pascoline envisions a university community that thrives on unity, innovation, and mutual respect. Through her leadership, she aims to continue fostering a progressive, inclusive, and dynamic environment that uplifts students academically, socially, and personally.

Bernadette Maureen Seiyan - Gender & Disability Mainstreaming Secretary

Bernadette Seiyan is a second-year Actuarial Science student at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, currently serving as the Gender and Disability Mainstreaming Secretary.

Known for her empathy, confidence and strong voice within the student community, Seiyan stepped into leadership driven by a genuine desire to make campus a more supportive and inclusive space for every student.

Her passion for gender equity and disability awareness comes from seeing the real challenges students face daily and wanting to make their experience a little easier and a lot fairer and her belief that every student deserves to feel safe, respected and represented. Seiyan brings a fresh perspective to leadership; one that values listening, understanding and creating practical, student-centered solutions.

Focused, approachable and deeply committed to fairness, she continues to use her role to uplift others and build a campus culture where diversity is embraced and no one feels left behind and yes, she somehow manages to do all this while still pretending she understands every line of her Actuarial notes.

Kevin Cheruiyot Rutto - Secretary General

Kevin Cheruiyot Rutto is a dedicated Bachelor of Science in Nursing student at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, known for his commitment to student leadership, service, and institutional development. His journey in student governance began with his successful election as a delegate in 2024, a role through which he demonstrated exceptional diligence, diplomacy, and a deep passion for representing student interests.

His consistency, competence, and calm but firm leadership approach earned him the trust of the student body, culminating in his current position as Secretary General of the Dedan Kimathi University of Technology Students’ Organization (DeKUTSO) and a Treasurer in the DeKUT Nursing Students’ Association(DeKUTNSA). As Secretary General, Kevin serves as the administrative backbone of the council, ensuring seamless communication, fostering a culture of accountability, coordinating council activities, and safeguarding the integrity of student governance processes.

Driven by a service-oriented mindset rooted in nursing and community care, Kevin brings a unique blend of empathy, strategic thinking, and organizational excellence to his leadership. He believes in building bridges, amplifying student voices, and creating sustainable structures that outlive individual terms.

Kevin’s vision for DeKUTSO centres on transparency, inclusive representation, and the continuous improvement of student welfare. His leadership is marked by humility, reliability, and an unwavering commitment to advancing the interests of every student at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology.

John Muchai - Sports, Entertainment and Security Secretary

John Muchai is a dedicated Mechanical Engineering student whose academic journey is complemented by strong leadership, cultural engagement, and active participation in university sports. He is widely recognized for his discipline, humility, and commitment to creating positive impact within and beyond DeKUT.

Leadership & Community Service

John serves as the President of Compassion Kenya – KE 0701 Runogone CDC, where he leads youth empowerment and mentorship programs designed to uplift communities.

In 2024, he also served as the President of the CIT (Compassion International Trainees) Short Courses at Meru University, coordinating leadership and capacity-building programs between April and August. His leadership across multiple institutions reflects his ability to unite young people and inspire meaningful change.

Sports & Campus Engagement

John is a goalkeeper for the DeKUT football team, a role that has given him firsthand experience with the challenges faced by student-athletes.

Being actively on the ground — in training, competitions, and team activities — inspired his passion for advocating fair support, better resources, and improved opportunities for all comrades.

He also holds the title of Mr. Culture DeKUT 2025/2026, representing the university with confidence, cultural awareness, and pride.

Vision & Values

John strongly believes the essence of leadership lies in service, integrity, and empowerment. His guiding principle is:
“Leadership is not about position — it is about purpose.”

Future Aspirations

John aims to apply his engineering knowledge and leadership experience to contribute to Kenya’s industrial advancement and youth development. He envisions creating platforms that empower young people, strengthen communities, and promote innovation.

Abigael Barongo Uhuru - Treasurer

Abigael Barongo Uhuru is a dedicated third-year student perusing Bachelor of Commerce, Accounting option at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology. She is known for her passion for financial management, transparency, and student empowerment. Her commitment to academic excellence, service, and integrity has shaped her into a grounded leader who consistently demonstrates professionalism and accountability in all her engagements.

Elected as the Treasurer of the Dedan Kimathi University of Technology Student Council 2025/2026, Abigael plays a critical role in managing student resources, driving transparent budgeting processes, and strengthening financial reporting systems. She champions clear communication, responsible allocation of funds, and improved financial structures that support student welfare, departmental needs, and sustainable campus initiatives. Her leadership reflects both competence and an unwavering resolve to ensure that students’ financial interests are protected and advanced.

With her strong background in Accounting, Abigael brings analytical expertise, ethical judgment, and strategic financial planning to university governance. She is committed to building a transparent, inclusive, and student-centered financial environment where every student feels informed, supported, and empowered. Abigael’s vision is a progressive and accountable student community that thrives on collaboration, innovation, and respect—uplifting students academically, socially and financially.

Anne Beryl Mwithaga - Campuses’ Secretary

Anne Beryl Mwithaga is a third-year student pursuing a BSc in Criminology and Security Management.

In my professional career as a leader, I have been able to mentor and guide my juniors in leadership roles and responsibilities while maintaining institutional values and professionalism.

Anne joins the student council as the Nairobi City Campuses’ Secretary, taking on this responsibility with deep humility, unwavering commitment, and a clear sense of the expectations placed upon her.

As I step into this role, I pay my highest tribute to Dr. Dennis Miano, Director of the Nairobi City Campus, for his mentorship. I am reminded that leadership is not just about leading, but about how we think, how we act, and how we hold ourselves accountable as a team and as individuals in delivering our shared goals.

Anne is committed to deepening a leadership culture that prioritizes service delivery, enhances student welfare, strengthens communication between students, student leaders, and the administration, while building a strong collaborative foundation for a thriving campus where everyone receives value for their money.

Having a reputation for being proactive, she is dedicated to sharing ideas, supporting one another, and building initiatives that enhance the academic, social, and welfare environment across our campuses and within the entire DeKUT family.

To the students, thank you once again for your trust. I promise to serve you faithfully, uphold your interests, and ensure that your voices continue to shape our campus.
