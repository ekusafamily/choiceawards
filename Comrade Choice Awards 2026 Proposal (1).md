## COMRADE CHOICE AWARDS 2026

## An Awards Initiative for Dedan Kimathi University of Technology

Official Platform: dekutso.com

Your Voice. Your Choice. Your Campus.

## Introduction

The Comrade Choice Awards is a proposed student awards initiative.

The awards will be hosted through dekutso.com, providing a central online platform for nominations, nominee profiles, voting, announcements, and final results.

## 2. Nomination Process

Students will be given an opportunity to nominate eligible individuals and organizations for the various categories.

A nomination may include:

- Name of nominee

- Course/programme

- Year of study

- Category

- Photograph

- Short profile

- Achievements

- Reason for nomination

Submitted nominations will undergo a review process before nominees are officially published.

This will help maintain the credibility and integrity of the awards.

## 3. Voting System

The Comrade Choice Awards will use a Point-based voting system.

Students and eligible participants will be able to support nominees by purchasing votes.

## Proposed minimum voting amount:

## KSh 10

KSh 10 → 10 points

KSh 50 → 50 points

KSh above 100 → 1.1 vote per 1 shilling


All payments and corresponding votes will be recorded to ensure accountability throughout the voting period.

## 4. Nominee Profiles

Each approved nominee will receive a dedicated profile on dekutso.com.

A nominee profile may contain:

- Name

- Course

- Year of study

- Award category

- Profile photograph

- Biography

- Achievements

- Voting information

The profile can then be shared by nominees through WhatsApp and other social media platforms.

This gives every nominee an opportunity to introduce themselves and showcase their contribution to the DeKUT community.

## 5. Voting & Leaderboards

During the voting period, students will be able to view the progress of nominees within their respective categories.

## Example:

## CLASS REPRESENTATIVE OF THE YEAR

| Position Nominee points |
| --- |
| 1 Jane Doe 2,840 2 John Doe 2,410 3 Mary Smith 1,975 |

Leaderboards will create engagement throughout the voting period while allowing students to follow the progress of their preferred nominees.


Final results will be determined after voting closes and the results have been verified.

Promotion can be carried out through:

- Instagram

- TikTok

- Facebook

- X

- Student associations

- Clubs

- Class groups

- Campus events

- Posters and other promotional materials

Nominees will also be encouraged to share their profiles with their supporters.

## 7. Domain vote.dekutso.com

Vote.dekutso.com will serve as the official online platform for the Comrade Choice Awards.

Students will be able to use the platform to:

- Learn about the awards

- View categories

- Submit nominations

- View nominees

- Read nominee profiles

- Vote

- Follow voting standings

- Access the awards rules


- Receive announcements

- View final results

The platform also provides an opportunity for the awards initiative to develop into a recurring annual student event.
